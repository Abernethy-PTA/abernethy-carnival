Carnival is a beloved school tradition and HUGE celebration for Abernethy students and the wider community. It officially kicks off with the sound of the bell at 5:30 PM for all families and ends with the sound of the bell at 8:00 PM. Families can enjoy traditional carnival games and prizes, inflatable obstacle courses, musical cakes, face-painting, balloon animals, and a photo booth. Food trucks offer a selection of food and treats — grab your dinner, have a seat at the nearby tables, or bring picnic blankets and chairs for lounging in the grassy area. A flat fee is charged for kids' admission, which buys each participant a wristband and allows kids to move freely between games and activities all night long. Wristbands do not cover fairy hair but do cover the rest of the games and activities.
This year is our 3rd annual Carnival pregame! The pregame is for kiddos in 2nd grade and younger only. The games and a few inflatables will be open, as well as Cake-a-Palooza in the auditorium. This gives the younger kids the opportunity to check out the Carnival without the craziest of the older, more seasoned kiddos.

Carnival's primary function is to bring our community together to celebrate. It is not a traditional fundraiser. The carnival committee does its best to price wristbands appropriately to cover expenses of the event itself. Any additional proceeds go directly to the PTA general fund, where they are used to enhance school programs.
Carnival is a true community event and relies heavily on parents and community participation. Consider volunteering to help set up, run a carnival game, bag up cotton candy, or sell wristbands. Sign up by scanning the QR code on the carnival flier or clicking this link.

If you aren't able to volunteer, consider dropping off a store-bought cake or baked good for the VERY POPULAR Musical Cake-a-Palooza. Donations will be collected in the auditorium the day of the event. Questions about Cake-a-Palooza? cake@supportabernethy.org
