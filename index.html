No. Adults do not need to purchase a wristband. We need you to supervise the games as a volunteer!
Wristbands can be purchased online through Konstella or at the school gazebo before & after school May 11–15, where cash and check are accepted.
Wristbands can be picked up at the gazebo before & after school May 11–15 or during the carnival.
In addition to unlimited games and activities, each wristband includes one cotton candy, one popcorn, and one donut. Wristbands do not cover food purchased from third-party vendors (pizza, shaved ice, etc.) or Fairy Sparkle hair services.
We want every child to enjoy Carnival regardless of ability to pay! Please contact counselor Jenny Morgan at jmorgan1@pps.net if you or someone you know needs assistance.
Carnival's primary function is to bring our community together to celebrate. It is not a traditional fundraiser. The carnival committee prices wristbands to cover expenses. Any proceeds go directly to the PTA general fund to enhance school programs.
Sign up on Konstella to help set up, run a carnival game, bag cotton candy, or sell wristbands. Questions? Email carnivalhelp@supportabernethy.org.
Drop off a store-bought cake or baked good in the auditorium the day of the event. Questions? Email cake@supportabernethy.org.
